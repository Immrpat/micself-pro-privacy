Privacy Policy

MicSelf Pro (com.namib.micselfpro)
Publisher: Immrpat
Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy describes how Immrpat (“we”) handles information in the Android app MicSelf Pro. It applies only to this app. MicSelf Pro is a paid vocal-monitor and practice-recording app. It has no account system, no advertising, and no network permission, so it cannot upload your audio to us.

Information stored on your device

MicSelf Pro keeps the information below in the app’s private storage on your phone. We do not receive a copy.

  • Microphone audio. The app asks for microphone permission so you can monitor your voice, see pitch, and record a take. Audio is processed on the device while those features are on. The app does not upload microphone audio.
  • Imported backing tracks. When you pick a file with Android’s file picker, the app copies that file into its private storage so it can play the track locally.
  • Practice recordings. Takes you record are saved in the app’s private storage, including the audio file, title, duration, date, and any moment flags you add.
  • Library and settings. The app stores titles, durations, dates, last-played time, and your audio settings (such as mic level, music level, reverb, pitch display, recording mode, and the selected track) so the app can run. These stay on the device.

The app does not collect an account, your name, your contacts, your location, an advertising identifier, analytics events, or crash reports for a server. It does not include an advertising or analytics SDK.

When audio leaves the app

Audio leaves the app’s private storage only when you ask it to.

  • Share. If you share a take, Android gives the app you choose temporary permission to read that file. MicSelf Pro does not send the file to our server.
  • Export. If you export a take, the app copies it to the Music/MicSelf Pro folder through Android’s media library. That copy is outside the app’s private storage. Other apps can reach it according to your device settings. MicSelf Pro does not send the exported file anywhere itself.

We do not sell microphone audio, recordings, imported tracks, or library information.

Android backup

Imported tracks, practice recordings, and the app’s library database are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Audio settings stored by the app may still be included in Android backup if you have backup turned on for the device. Android and the device maker apply their own backup rules to anything that is not excluded.

How long information stays

You can delete an imported track or a recording in the app. That removes the app-private copy and its library record. Uninstalling MicSelf Pro removes its private files, database, and settings from the device.

A file you exported to Music/MicSelf Pro stays on the device after you delete the take in the app or uninstall MicSelf Pro. Remove that copy with your phone’s file or music tools.

We do not keep your audio on a server, so there is no server copy to delete. Email pat23.w@gmail.com if you have a privacy question.

Children

MicSelf Pro is not directed at children under 13. It does not offer accounts and does not knowingly collect personal information from children on a server.

Security

App-private audio and library data stay in Android’s private app storage. No method of storage is completely secure. The app is designed to limit collection by processing audio on the device and by not operating an account, advertising, or cloud-audio service.

Changes

We may update this page when the app’s data practices change. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.
